A note from the team

Why we’re inviting Google to partner with Elan & nVision 2027.

Dear Google team,

Every January, IIT Hyderabad’s vibrant campus turns into a 27,000-square-metre stage for Elan & nVision, our joint cultural and technical festival. Over four days, tens of thousands of students from across India build, code, compete, and discover.

With Google’s pioneering culture in AI, cloud, and developer empowerment, we would love to collaborate on flagship hackathons, tech talks, and developer student experiences.

We’d love ten minutes of your team’s time to talk through what fits best.
